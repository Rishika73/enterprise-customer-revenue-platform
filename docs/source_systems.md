# Source Systems

## CRM
Customer and account master data.

Primary entities:
- customers
- account_owners

## Billing
Subscription and payment information.

Primary entities:
- subscriptions
- transactions

## Product Analytics
Customer product usage and engagement events.

Primary entities:
- product_usage

## Support
Customer support incidents and ticket history.

Primary entities:
- support_tickets

## Customer Success
Customer health scores and renewal risk indicators.

Primary entities:
- account_health

## Product Catalog
Products, plans, and pricing information.

Primary entities:
- products
